About AgriMitra

AgriMitra is an open, technology-driven market intelligence and agricultural trade platform built to solve the fundamental question for producers:

“What should I sell, where should I sell it, when should I sell it, and who should I sell it to?”

By bringing together verified farmers, FPOs, wholesale buyers, logistics partners, and transparent bank settlement, AgriMitra reduces price volatility and post-harvest losses.
